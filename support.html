Common questions

I forgot my password — how do I reset it?

On the login screen, tap "Forgot password" and follow the OTP reset steps sent to your registered email.

My coach can see my progress photos — is that expected?

Yes. Progress photos and measurements you upload are visible to the coach assigned to your account so they can guide your training. They are not visible to other members.

How do I cancel my membership or request a refund?

Contact us at theconquerclubdev@gmail.com with your account details and we'll help you with cancellations, billing, or refund questions.

How do I delete my account and data?

Email us from your registered address requesting account deletion. We'll confirm once your data has been removed, per our Privacy Policy.

Report a bug

Found something broken? Email us with your device type, app version, and a short description of what happened — screenshots help a lot.
