Account & Data Deletion

This page provides the public account and data deletion instructions for THE CONQUER CLUB.

How to delete your account

1. Open the THE CONQUER CLUB App.

2. Open Settings.

3. Select Delete Account.

4. Confirm the deletion request.

Your request will be processed by THE CONQUER CLUB within 48 hours of verification. Personal data that we are not required or permitted to retain will be deleted as part of the account deletion process. Certain information may be retained where required for legal, accounting, fraud-prevention, dispute-resolution, security, or other legitimate legal purposes, as described in our Privacy Policy.

Cannot access the App?

If you cannot access your account, email theconquerclubdev@gmail.com and clearly state that you want to delete your THE CONQUER CLUB account and associated personal data. We may need to verify account ownership before processing the request.

Common questions

I forgot my password — how do I reset it?

On the login screen, tap "Forgot password" and follow the OTP reset steps sent to your registered email.

My coach can see my progress photos — is that expected?

Yes. Progress photos and measurements you upload are visible to the coach assigned to your account so they can guide your training. They are not intended to be visible to other members.

How do I cancel my membership or request a refund?

Contact us at theconquerclubdev@gmail.com with your account details and we'll help with cancellation, billing, or refund questions. Any refund will be handled according to the membership/payment terms communicated at the time of purchase.

Report a bug

Found something broken? Email us with your device type, app version, and a short description of what happened — screenshots help a lot.
