Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information THE CONQUER CLUB ("the App", "we", "us") collects, how it is used, how it is shared, how long it is retained, and the choices available to you. It applies to the THE CONQUER CLUB mobile app on Android and iOS and the services it connects to.

Who we are

THE CONQUER CLUB is a training and coaching app. If you have questions about this policy or your data, contact us at theconquerclubdev@gmail.com.

Information we collect

Depending on how you use the App as a member, coach, or admin, we may collect:

CategoryExamples
Account detailsName, email address, phone number, and account credentials. Passwords are handled by the authentication service and are not stored by us in plain text.
Profile & body dataBody measurements, weight, and progress photos you upload.
Training dataWorkout plans, exercises, workout completion, and workout session history.
Nutrition dataDiet plans assigned to you and diet tracking entries.
Activity dataStep count, daily streaks, and related activity history. If you grant health permissions, the App may read step-count data from the supported health service on your device.
Payment informationMembership and payment records associated with your account. Payments are currently made manually via UPI or bank transfer and confirmed by our team; the App does not process card payments and we do not request or store full card numbers.
Coach–member dataIf you are a member, your assigned coach can view the training, nutrition, measurements, progress photos, and activity data needed to provide coaching.
Device and technical informationApp version, device type, operating-system information, and diagnostic or crash information where available.

Health and fitness data

The App may process fitness-related information such as step counts, body measurements, weight, workout history, nutrition information, and progress photos. If you grant permission to access health data through a supported health service, the App uses that permission for the fitness features that require it. Health and fitness information is sensitive and is not sold for advertising.

How we use your information

  • To create and manage your account and role (member, coach, or admin).
  • To deliver workout plans, diet plans, and progress tracking.
  • To let coaches review and guide the members assigned to them.
  • To maintain streaks, activity history, measurements, and progress records.
  • To maintain membership and payment records.
  • To provide health and fitness features when you grant the required device permissions.
  • To keep the App secure, diagnose issues, and improve reliability and performance.

Where your data is stored

App data, including account-related records and uploaded progress photos, is stored and processed using Supabase, including its database, authentication, and file-storage services used by the App. Cloudflare may be used for website, hosting, delivery, or related infrastructure services where applicable. Data transmitted between the App and supported services is protected using encrypted HTTPS connections.

Who we share data with

  • Your coach, if you're a member — they can see the training, nutrition, measurements, progress photos, and activity data needed to coach you.
  • Service providers that provide infrastructure or technical services used to operate the App, including Supabase and Cloudflare where applicable.
  • Payment service providers — membership payments are currently collected manually via UPI/bank transfer and verified by our team. We do not currently use an automated in-app payment gateway. If we add one (for example, a UPI-based gateway) in the future, this Policy will be updated and the gateway provider will handle payment details according to its own terms and privacy policy.
  • We do not sell your personal data to third parties.
  • We may disclose information when required by law or when reasonably necessary to protect the rights, security, or safety of users or the service.

Progress photos

Photos you upload to track your progress are stored in private application storage and are intended to be accessible only to you and your assigned coach according to the App's access controls. You can request deletion of your photos or your account using the deletion process described below.

Data retention

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the App and maintain your account. When you submit a valid account deletion request, we delete personal data that we are not required or permitted to retain within 48 hours of verifying the request. Some information may be retained where required for legal, accounting, fraud-prevention, dispute-resolution, security, or other legitimate legal purposes. Where information must be retained, access and use will be limited to the purpose requiring retention.

Your choices

  • Access & correction — update supported profile information directly in the App or contact us for assistance.
  • Deletion — request account and associated data deletion directly in the App (Settings → Delete Account). You can also use our Account & Data Deletion instructions or email theconquerclubdev@gmail.com.
  • Health permissions — where supported by your device, you can manage health permissions through the device's health/privacy settings. Removing a permission may prevent related App features from working.
  • Export — request a copy of available personal data by contacting us.

Account deletion

Account deletion can be initiated from Settings → Delete Account in the App. If you cannot access the App, contact us at theconquerclubdev@gmail.com. We will verify and process a valid deletion request and remove personal data that we are not required to retain within 48 hours, subject to the retention exceptions described above. For instructions, see Support & Account Deletion.

Children's privacy

THE CONQUER CLUB is intended for users who are at least 16 years old. We do not knowingly allow children below the applicable minimum age to create accounts.

Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures, including encrypted connections, access controls, authentication controls, and secure cloud services, to protect your information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We'll update the "Last updated" date above when material changes are made and, where appropriate, provide notice within the App.
