Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information THE CONQUER CLUB ("the App", "we", "us") collects, how it is used, and the choices you have. It applies to the THE CONQUER CLUB mobile app on Android and iOS and the services it connects to.

Who we are

THE CONQUER CLUB is a training and coaching app. If you have questions about this policy or your data, contact us at theconquerclubdev@gmail.com.

Information we collect

Depending on how you use the App as a member, coach, or admin, we collect:

CategoryExamples
Account detailsName, email address, phone number, password (stored securely, never in plain text)
Profile & body dataBody measurements, weight, progress photos you upload
Training dataWorkout plans, exercises completed, workout session history
Nutrition dataDiet plans assigned to you and diet tracking entries
Activity dataStep count, daily streaks, app usage patterns
Payment informationMembership/payment records handled through our payment processor (we do not store full card numbers)
Coach–member dataIf you are a member, your assigned coach can view the training, nutrition, and progress data above
Device informationApp version, device type, and diagnostic/crash data

How we use your information

  • To create and manage your account and role (member, coach, or admin)
  • To deliver workout plans, diet plans, and progress tracking
  • To let coaches review and guide the members assigned to them
  • To process membership payments
  • To maintain streaks and activity history
  • To keep the App secure, diagnose issues, and improve performance

Where your data is stored

App data, including progress photos, is stored using Supabase (secure cloud database and file storage), and our backend runs on Cloudflare Workers. Data is transmitted using encrypted (HTTPS) connections.

Who we share data with

  • Your coach, if you're a member — they can see the training and progress data needed to coach you.
  • Service providers that host our infrastructure (Supabase, Cloudflare) and process payments, strictly to operate the App.
  • We do not sell your personal data to third parties.
  • We may disclose information if required by law or to protect the rights and safety of our users.

Progress photos

Photos you upload to track your progress are stored in a private storage bucket and are only visible to you and your assigned coach. You can request deletion of your photos at any time (see "Your choices" below).

Data retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, we delete or anonymize your personal data within a reasonable time, except where we're required to keep records for legal or accounting reasons.

Your choices

  • Access & correction — update your profile information directly in the App.
  • Deletion — request account and data deletion by contacting theconquerclubdev@gmail.com.
  • Export — request a copy of your data by contacting us.

Children's privacy

THE CONQUER CLUB is not directed at children under 13 (or under 16 where required by local law), and we do not knowingly collect data from children in that age range.

Security

We use industry-standard measures — encrypted connections, access controls, and secure cloud storage — to protect your information. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, but we work to protect your data appropriately.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We'll update the "Last updated" date above when we do, and post material changes within the App.

Contact us

Questions about this policy or your data? Reach us at theconquerclubdev@gmail.com or call 077758 93596.
